Provenance and risk

Registry verified. Published from the oaslananka account, and the linked repository sits under the same account. Namespace ownership was proven at publish time.

The record references an OAuth flow, so expect a browser consent step rather than a static key. Confirm the scopes on the consent screen before you approve.

A listing in the official registry is a namespace ownership check, not a security review. ConnectorZone does not execute connectors and does not audit their code. Treat every connector as third party software with the access you grant it.
