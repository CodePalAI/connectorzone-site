Provenance and risk

Registry verified. Published under the domain namespace com.knitli. The registry required proof of control over that domain before accepting the record.

A long lived key or token is required. Scope it as narrowly as the upstream service allows and rotate it on a schedule.

A listing in the official registry is a namespace ownership check, not a security review. ConnectorZone does not execute connectors and does not audit their code. Treat every connector as third party software with the access you grant it.
