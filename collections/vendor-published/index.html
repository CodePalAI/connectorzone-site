Connectors published by the vendor

The registry verifies namespace ownership two ways. A name beginning io.github means somebody proved control of that account. A name built from a domain means somebody proved control of that domain. When the domain also matches the product website on the record, you are almost certainly looking at software published by the company that operates the service behind it.

Selection ruleNamespace is a verified domain rather than a personal account, and that domain matches the published website.

Before you attach any of these

  • Vendor published does not mean audited. It means the publisher is who they say they are.
  • A vendor connector still runs with whatever access you grant it, and vendors ship bugs like everyone else.
  • Check the version and the last update date. An official connector that has not moved in a year is still stale.

Membership is recalculated from the registry snapshot on every build, so a connector can enter or leave this set when its record changes. Read thesecurity guide before granting access to anything sensitive.
