Categories

The registry has no reliable category field, so ConnectorZone derives one from each record. The rules are published, and the group with the most members is the one where the rules found nothing specific to match.

  • General utilities

    5,296

    Focused single purpose connectors that do not sit inside a larger group.

  • Commerce and finance

    Payments, billing, accounting, trading, banking, crypto and market data.

Classification is keyword based and imperfect. A connector sits in exactly one primary category, chosen by the highest priority rule it matches. The full rule set is in the repository and summarised on the methodology page.
