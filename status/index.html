Known limits

  • This is a snapshot. Connectors published after 2 August 2026 at 04:28 UTC are not here yet. The sync runs on a schedule and only rewrites the snapshot when the upstream content changes.
  • Categories are keyword derived and imperfect. A connector sits in exactly one primary category, chosen by the highest priority rule it matches.
  • Compatibility verdicts are derived from documented client behaviour, not from executing connectors. Nothing on this site reports a tested install.
  • Publisher descriptions vary enormously in quality. A short description lowers a completeness score without saying anything about the software behind it.
  • Health and uptime of hosted endpoints are not measured. A hosted endpoint listed here may be offline.

Machine readable versions of these numbers are available from thedata endpoints, and the rules behind them are on themethodology page.
